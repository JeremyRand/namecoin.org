Recently, 6 Namecoin developers became aware of past activity by our former UX engineer, Zachary Lym (AKA indolering), that we cannot ethically condone. Zach has not been actively involved in Namecoin for years, which is sufficient reason on its own to remove him from the Team page; as such, a complete investigation of the aforementioned past activity is not a productive usage of our resources. However, we feel that it is important to state that, as a result of this activity being brought to our attention, we will oppose any potential future involvement of Zach with Namecoin development, and we do not consider Zach welcome in our spaces going forward. If you wish to verify authenticity of any Namecoin-related applications, projects, or communications, please contact Daniel Kraft or Jeremy Rand via OpenPGP-signed email. We do not expect to make any additional statements on this subject.
